The Person Behind the Work

Not who I planned
to be — better.
Zoe Beatrice R. Ceniza
21 yrs old March 19, 2005 Parañaque City

Choosing BSIT was never the plan. The original dream? Dermatologist. Psychiatrist. Someone who fixes people from the inside out. But life, in its signature chaotic fashion, had a different redirect queued up and honestly? Best. Bug. Ever.

Turns out, there's something deeply satisfying about debugging a broken loop at midnight, watching a UI come alive from nothing but code, and leading a team to ship something real. The logic, the creativity, the controlled chaos of it all, she found her thing.

Today, Zoe designs mockups in Figma, builds interfaces in HTML, and aspires to grow into a Project Manager or Database Manager someone who sits at the intersection of people, systems, and vision.

✈ Travel 🎮 Games ✦ Figma ✦ Canva 💻 Web Dev
Iskolar ng Bayan — PUP Parañaque campus, making every peso of tuition count.
Started with medicine in mind, ended up making systems instead and honestly? No regrets whatsoever.
Leads teams, writes code, and reviews her own work. The rare triple-threat PM + Dev + QA combo.
